MY BACKGROUND

About the Founder

Combining industrial experience, engineering knowledge, and modern cloud technologies.

{{ founder.name }} is the Founder and Lead Developer of SIAS Hub (Smart Industrial Automation System), a technology project dedicated to advancing industrial automation, intelligent machine monitoring, and digital transformation.

He holds a Bachelor's degree in Industrial Mathematics and a Master's degree in Computer Engineering, specializing in Computer Systems and Networks.

His professional background combines Cloud Engineering, DevOps, industrial manufacturing, infrastructure automation, and software development. His technical experience includes Microsoft Azure, AWS, Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes, Python, and CI/CD technologies.

Through firsthand experience with industrial production equipment, including extrusion and laminating coating systems, he recognized opportunities to improve operational visibility, machine performance monitoring, fault detection, and maintenance processes.

These experiences inspired the creation of SIAS Hub: a project designed to explore how industrial equipment, cloud computing, and intelligent software can work together to support smarter manufacturing.

THE BIGGER PICTURE

The Vision Behind SIAS Hub

To bridge the gap between traditional industrial machinery and modern intelligent technologies, creating a future where manufacturing systems are more connected, efficient, reliable, and data-driven.

SIAS Hub aims to develop industrial monitoring and automation solutions that help organizations gain better visibility into their equipment, identify operational abnormalities, analyze production data, and make informed maintenance decisions.
